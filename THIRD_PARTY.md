# Third-party software

Binary releases of `kil` include parts of [KiCadRoutingTools](https://github.com/drandyhaas/KiCadRoutingTools):

- version: `v0.21.4`
- files: `py_router`, the platform-specific `grid_router` module, `Cargo.toml`, `VERSION`, and `requirements.txt`
- license: MIT

The original KiCadRoutingTools `LICENSE` file is stored as `lib/krt/LICENSE` in every `kil` release archive and installation.

KiCadRoutingTools is maintained by its own contributors. The `kil` project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the KiCad project or the KiCadRoutingTools maintainers.
